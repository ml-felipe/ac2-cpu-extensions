# Provenance and redistribution

Recovered from Felipe Martínez's UPC-FIB AC2 Drive materials:

- Lab4/Entrega: `branch_unit.sv`, `branch_prediction_checker.sv`, `datapath.sv`.
- Lab5: `alu.sv`, `decoder.sv` and the C/assembly sources in the test/performance archives.

The decoder and datapath retain their DRAC / Barcelona Supercomputing Center headers crediting Guillem Lopez Paradis. They contain upstream/course infrastructure as well as coursework changes. A matching pristine starter tree and collaborator history were unavailable, so no line-by-line original-authorship claim is made.

The test-only package fixture, regression testbench, Makefile and documentation were written during migration. The two program repairs are described in the README. No missing processor implementation has been fabricated. Compiled binaries, reports, handouts, duplicate archives and unrelated assets are omitted.

The repository owner confirmed on 24 November 2026 that they hold the necessary redistribution rights for the coursework and included third-party material. Original author credits and copyright notices are retained; no new license is assigned to inherited code.
