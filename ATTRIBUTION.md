# Provenance and redistribution

Recovered from Felipe Martínez's UPC-FIB AC2 Drive materials:

- Lab4/Entrega: `branch_unit.sv`, `branch_prediction_checker.sv`, `datapath.sv`.
- Lab5: `alu.sv`, `decoder.sv` and the C/assembly sources in the test/performance archives.

The decoder and datapath retain their DRAC / Barcelona Supercomputing Center headers crediting Guillem Lopez Paradis. They contain upstream/course infrastructure as well as coursework changes. Other recovered modules have no sufficiently clear license grant. A matching pristine starter tree and collaborator history were unavailable, so no line-by-line original-authorship claim is made.

The test-only package fixture, regression testbench, Makefile and documentation were written during migration. The two program repairs are described in the README. No missing processor implementation has been fabricated. Compiled binaries, reports, handouts, duplicate archives and unrelated assets are omitted.

Keep this repository private until the applicable DRAC version's redistribution terms, course-template terms and contributor attribution are established. No license is invented or applied to the inherited tree.
